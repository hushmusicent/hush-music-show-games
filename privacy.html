Privacy Policy — Hush Music Show Games

Effective date: November 23, 2026
Operated by: AppSolut Group

This Privacy Policy explains how Hush Music Show Games ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: username, email address, and password, used to create your account and let you sign in. You also get a player code you can share to add friends.
  • Gameplay data: points balance, scores, game history, and leaderboard entries.
  • Social features: friends list, friend requests, game room invitations, and in-game chat messages.
  • Device and technical data: device type, operating system version, and basic diagnostics collected automatically through our backend provider to keep the App running.
  • Microphone: if you use voice chat, your audio is transmitted in real time to other players in your game session over a peer-to-peer connection. We do not record or store voice audio.
  • Speech input: if you answer a question by speaking, your voice is processed by your device's built-in speech recognition to turn it into text. We do not record or store voice audio.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and keep it secure.
  • To run multiplayer matchmaking, rooms, leaderboards, and chat.
  • To prevent cheating, abuse, and fraud.
  • To improve the App and fix technical issues.

3. Virtual Points Disclosure

The App includes game shows where you can wager virtual points in player-vs-player rooms (winner takes the pot). These games use virtual points only. No real money is wagered, and points cannot be redeemed for cash, prizes, or anything of monetary value.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which process data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase — account sign-in and backend database. Google Privacy Policy

Voice chat uses WebRTC peer-to-peer connections directly between players' devices; no third-party server records or stores voice audio.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service provider above as needed to operate the App, or when required by law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below. We will delete or anonymize your data within a reasonable period unless we are required to retain it by law.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this page with a revised effective date.
